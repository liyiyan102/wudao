>> 出国上大师课，卡住你的往往不是动作，是听不懂老师那几句口令。workshop 比常规课更快：示范一遍就 5, 6, 7, 8，你慢半拍就跟丢。把下面整句背熟，比背单词管用 🎧

街舞大师课的英语翻来覆去就那几句。按一堂课顺序：进场、教动作、过 combo、纠错、下课社交。

## 进场、站位

- **Is this the check-in for the workshop?** 报到在这吗
- **I'm on the list / I registered online.** 我报过名了
- **Where should I put my bag?** 包放哪
- **Can I squeeze in here?** 我插这行行吗
- **Watch me first, don't dance yet.** 先看，别急着跳
- **I'm facing you, so your right is my left. Mirror me.** 镜面，别跟错边
- **When I turn around, same side as me.** 我转背面时跟同侧

前排留给能顶上的人。老师说 **switch lines**（换排）时主动往后让。

## 教动作

老师拆得快，常常边做边用动作名当拍子喊。

- **We're taking this 8-count only.** 就学这一组八拍
- **It goes: step, step, hit, hold.** 迈、迈、卡、停
- **Right foot first, then we sit.** 先右脚，再沉重心
- **The hit is on the 2, not the 1. Don't rush it.** 卡点在 2，别抢
- **It's on the "and" — the half beat.** 落在半拍
- **Hold 5, 6. Then hit 7, 8.** 5、6 定住，7、8 才动
- **Feet first. We'll add the arms after.** 先脚后手
- **Same thing, other side.** 反面再来

拟声跟感觉，不跟数字：

- **Boom on 1, ka on 2, tss-tss on the hats.** bass / snare / hi-hat
- **Ride the bass, don't pop every count.** 顺低音走，别每拍都砸

## 过 combo、分组 ✨

- **Let's take it from the top.** 从头过
- **Slow first, then tempo.** 先慢，再原速
- **With music. I'll count you in: 5, 6, 7, 8.** 放歌接
- **Mark it — 50 percent, just placements.** 半力走位置
- **Full out. Performance face.** 全力，表情给足
- **One more time, give it your all.** 再来一遍（通常不是最后一遍）
- **Groups. First group up, second group watch.** 分组过
- **Who wants to go in the front for the video?** 谁上前排拍视频

## 被点名、纠错

- **You — front and center, let's see it.** 你，上来跳一个
- **You're early. Wait for the snare.** 抢拍了
- **Stay in the pocket.** 贴着节奏，别拱
- **Get lower, weight in the heels.** 蹲下去
- **Isolate — only the chest.** 只动胸
- **Put a period on it. Clean picture.** 收住，定格干净
- **Milk it, don't cut it off.** 延着走
- **More attitude. It's not ballet.** 给点味道

你自己要会问：

- **Can you break down the last 8 again?** 最后八拍再拆一遍
- **Is the turn on 3 or on the "and"?** 转在 3 还是半拍
- **Which foot do I start on?** 哪只脚起
- **Can we run it one more time slow?** 再慢跑一遍

听不懂就直接问。闷着跟丢、还占前排才尴尬。错了别停：**if you mess up, keep going**。

## 下课：打招呼、换联系方式、合照 🕺

海外默认换 Instagram。WeChat 一般是华人班、或对方先提才用。顺序：先夸课 → 再留联系方式 → 最后合照。别一上来就像查户口，轻松一点更自然 🙂

### 打招呼

- **Hey, was this your first workshop with them?** 第一次上他的课吗
- **You killed that last 8.** 你最后那段太顶了
- **Have you been dancing long?** 跳多久了
- **Are you from around here or just here for the workshop?** 本地还是专程来的

### 上次看你比赛之后（夸人 + 深聊）

直译「我特别喜欢你」在英语里会发腻。更自然的说法是 **I've been a fan since…** / **I've really liked your dancing since…**。

- **I've really liked your dancing since I saw you last time.** 上次看你跳完，我就特别喜欢你的舞
- **I've been a fan since I saw you battle.** 看完那场 battle，我就粉上了
- **I saw you compete last time — your second round stuck with me.** 上次看你比赛，第二轮我到现在都记得
- **That freeze at the end was crazy.** 结尾那个 freeze 太疯了
- **Your musicality in that round was so clean.** 那一轮卡点特别干净
- **I've been checking your stuff since then.** 从那之后我一直在看你发的东西
- **I didn't know you were taking this workshop too.** 没想到你也来上这节

### 平时练多久、怎么练

- **How often do you practice?** 你平时多久练一次
- **How many hours a week do you usually train?** 一周大概练几小时
- **What's your practice routine like?** 你平时怎么练的
- **Do you train at a studio, or more on your own?** 去舞室练，还是自己练得多
- **Do you take class every week, or more like workshops?** 周常课多，还是 workshop 多
- **When you train, is it more foundations, choreo, or freestyle?** 练基本功、编舞，还是即兴
- **Do you film yourself a lot when you practice?** 练的时候会不会经常拍自己
- **How do you work on your groove / footwork?** 律动 / 脚步你怎么抠的
- **Any drills you always go back to?** 有没有你反复练的那几个 drill
- **Are you prepping for another battle?** 在备下一场吗
- **When's your next jam?** 下一场 jam 什么时候
- **Who do you usually train with?** 平时跟谁一起练
- **Can I come through next time you run sessions?** 下次你们开练我能去吗（熟一点再说）

问练习别一上来像采访。先夸一轮具体动作，再问 **How do you usually practice?** 对方才愿意讲。

### 换联系方式

- **What's your IG? I'll follow you.** 换 Ins（默认）
- **I'll DM you the class video.** 给个交换理由
- **Do you have WeChat too, or just Instagram?** 华人圈再问
- **Can I add you on WeChat? I'll send the recap.** 加好友要有由头
- **We're making a group chat for this piece — want in?** 建群

### 合照

- **Can we grab a pic real quick?** 合个影
- **Group photo — everybody in!** 全班合影
- **One with the teacher?** 跟老师合照（先问老师）
- **Say 5-6-7-8!** 喊口号按快门
- **Tag me if you post it — I'm @___.** 发帖记得 tag
- **Can I see it before you post?** 发之前给我看下

## 一套能直接用的下课对话

Hey — you were in the first group, that last 8 was clean. I'm ___. What's your IG? I can send you the recap video. Do you have WeChat too, or just Instagram? Cool — can we grab a pic real quick? Tag me if you post it.

刚那组最后八拍好干净。我叫 __。你 IG 是？我把视频发你。你有 WeChat 还是只用 Ins？合个影。发的话 tag 我。

## 碰见刚看过比赛的人

Hey — I saw you last time. I've really liked your dancing since that battle. Your second round was so clean. How often do you practice? What's your routine like — more foundations or freestyle? I'm ___. What's your IG?

嘿，上次看过你跳。那场之后我就特别喜欢你的舞。第二轮太干净了。你平时多久练一次？怎么练的，基本功多还是即兴多？我叫 __。你 IG 是？
